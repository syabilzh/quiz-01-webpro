Bekasi City
Kota Patriot

The very place I grew up in. Bekasi has one of the richest, most transformative lineages in the Indonesian archipelago, journeying from the sacred canal builders of ancient Tarumanegara to the fiery frontline defense of modern republic freedom.

Why is it called Kota Patriot?

Bekasi is called Kota Patriot (City of Patriots) because of the fierce bravery and sacrifice of its people in fighting Dutch colonial forces and Allied troops to defend Indonesia's independence after 1945.

Fun Facts about Bekasi

Bekasi is widely known as a satellite city because millions of its residents travel back and forth to Jakarta daily for work or university, making it one of the busiest commuter hubs in the metropolitan area.

Bekasi famously went viral as a joke among Indonesian netizens who playfully referred to it as a "separate planet" due to its distance from central Jakarta and warm weather. In reality, it has rapidly evolved into a modern metropolis equipped with high-end infrastructure like LRT lines.

Hidden behind its residential growth, the Cikarang area in Bekasi is home to one of the largest industrial manufacturing hubs in all of Southeast Asia.
