Tourist spots in Bekasi

Gedung Juang 45

Bekasi's independence struggle history education center has been revitalized with modern interactive multimedia technology and immersive exhibitions.

It serves as an iconic cultural landmark that bridges historical preservation with modern digital storytelling, making it a must-visit educational destination for visitors of all ages.

Hutan Kota & Taman Rusa

A green lung in the heart of the city with shady trees, a comfortable jogging track, and a family-friendly spotted deer conservation area.

It provides a refreshing natural escape from the busy metropolitan atmosphere, allowing residents and tourists to relax, exercise, and interact gently with nature.
