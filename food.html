Craving the local tastes?

Here’s what Bekasi has to offer

Gabus Pucung

This legendary dish has roots in the traditions of the native Betawi communities living along the rivers of Bekasi, where the local rivers and swamps were once abundant with wild snakehead fish (ikan gabus).

It features a deep black, savory broth rich in spices and flavored distinctively with pucung (kluwek) seeds, paired with the firm yet tender texture of the snakehead fish.

Bubur Tambun

Bubur Tambun is deeply tied to the local Betawi-Sundanese culinary heritage of the Bekasi region, featuring a rich, savory broth, traditional local spices, and distinct rustic toppings.

A famous running joke among locals describes the legendary Bubur Tambun vendors as interplanetary speed racers: "If you're slightly late, Jakarta starves!" They are known to speed aggressively toward the capital at dawn just to make sure office workers don't miss their breakfast mission.
